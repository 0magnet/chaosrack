Every model

Every one of the 76 systems chaosrack draws: continuous flows, discrete maps, parametric figures, geometry and live-audio embeddings — each with its equations and a link that opens it running in the browser. They are listed in the order the model-selector knob turns through them, which is the order they are in the app. A model that appears under two categories is shown under both, because that is what the selector does.
