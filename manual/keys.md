## Keys

<!-- key: keys -->
Keys — a playable polyphonic keyboard: click or drag the keybed (glissando works), or play the computer keyboard on the labeled keys (Z row = lower octave, Q row = upper). The bed is the whole piano, A0 to C8, across the whole bay; what it sounds like, how loud and on which speakers is the Synth bay above it.

### Piano keybed

<!-- key: keys-bed -->
Piano keybed — click or hold-and-slide to play; the letter-labeled keys map to your computer keyboard
