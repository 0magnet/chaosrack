# Generators

## Gen 4

<!-- key: gen-v -->
Gen 4 — a generator, for sound or for modulation. The FREQ knob (log, one turn per octave) over a piano octave; the level knob inside its envelope's ATTACK and DECAY rings, with ENV between them to repeat it; and the waveform knob, OFF at the bottom, with SOLO beside it to hear only this one and OUT under it.

Where it goes is OUT: OFF, L, C (both sides) or R, on the speakers and the rack's signal together. The Mixer's GEN 4 column has every other pinning — the speakers or the rack alone, a gain, an inverted side, the sends to modulation — and OUT reads MIX while it does.

### Gen 4 frequency in Hz

<!-- key: gen-v-led -->
Gen 4 frequency in Hz — type a value or turn the knob (log scale, one turn per octave)

<!-- key: rst-gen-v-freq -->
Reset the frequency

<!-- key: gen-v-lvl-led -->
Gen 4 level (0–100)

### Gen 4 envelope attack

<!-- key: gen-v-atk -->
Gen 4 envelope attack — the rise from silence to its level, in milliseconds

### Gen 4 envelope decay

<!-- key: gen-v-dcy -->
Gen 4 envelope decay — the fall from its level back to silence, in milliseconds

### Gen 4 envelope

<!-- key: gen-v-env -->
Gen 4 envelope — off: steady at its level; rpt: attack then decay, over and over, a shaped tremolo

<!-- key: rst-gen-v-lvl -->
Reset the level

### Gen 4 waveform

<!-- key: gen-v-wave -->
Gen 4 waveform — what it plays, OFF at the bottom and then sine, triangle, square, saw, shift-register noise and the four test stimuli round the ring

### Gen 4 out

<!-- key: gen-v-out -->
Gen 4 out — where it goes: OFF, L, C (both sides) or R, on the speakers and the rack's signal together; MIX when the Mixer has it pinned some other way. The display beside it names it; a click on the display lists the positions.

### WAVE positions

- <!-- key: gen-wave=sine --> sine — one pure frequency and no harmonics at all
- <!-- key: gen-wave=tri --> triangle — odd harmonics falling away steeply: soft and hollow
- <!-- key: gen-wave=sqr --> square — odd harmonics falling away slowly: hollow and reedy
- <!-- key: gen-wave=saw --> sawtooth — every harmonic, the brightest of the four
- <!-- key: gen-wave=noise --> noise — a 15-bit shift register; the freq knob sets its playback rate
