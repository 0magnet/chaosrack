# Scopes

## Scope 1

<!-- key: scope -->
Scope 1 — one of the rack's four oscilloscopes, each beside its generator. Under VOLTS, the two INPUT knobs, 1 and 2, pick what each channel is fed from — the rack's signal (RACK L or R, as the Mixer makes it), the capture (CAP L or R, wherever the Mixer sends it), one generator by itself, or a coordinate of the model (Model Out) — named on the face. The tube, and at its right the SOURCE buttons — what the beam draws — between the small H and V knobs that move the trace sideways and up and down. Under it the beam's knob: its outer ring is SCALE ILLUM, which lights the graticule and, turned down past the bottom of its travel, clicks into OFF and switches the scope off; inside it INTENSITY, and innermost FOCUS. Beside that the TRIGGER, with its SLOPE and MODE buttons, and below them VOLTS and TIME, how much a division is worth up and across.

### The tube

<!-- key: scope-screen-cell -->
The tube — ten divisions by eight. SCALE ILLUM lights the graticule and switches the scope on and off.

### Horizontal position

<!-- key: rst-scope-hpos-cell -->
Horizontal position — slides the swept window earlier or later within the audio that was captured, in divisions.

Reset the horizontal position

### Vertical position

<!-- key: rst-scope-vpos-cell -->
Vertical position — moves the trace up or down the screen, in divisions. It shifts where the trace is drawn, not the signal.

<!-- key: rst-scope-vpos -->
Reset the vertical position

<!-- key: scope-intens-led-cell -->
The beam. The knob is INTENSITY: beam brightness, and how long the phosphor holds the trace. Under it, two mini knobs. I is SCALE ILLUM, which lights the graticule; turned down past the bottom of its travel it clicks into OFF and switches the scope off — the tube goes dark and costs the rack nothing. F is FOCUS: how tight the spot is; off focus the trace blurs, the way a real tube does. The display reads the intensity, or whichever mini knob is being turned.

### Intensity, 0–1

<!-- key: scope-intens-led -->
Intensity, 0–1 — type a value or turn the middle ring

### Scale illumination

<!-- key: scope-illum -->
Scale illumination — lights the graticule. Turned down past the bottom of its travel it clicks into OFF, and the scope is off.

### Intensity

<!-- key: scope-intens -->
Intensity — beam brightness, and how long the phosphor holds the trace

### Focus

<!-- key: scope-focus -->
Focus — how tight the spot is: sharpest at the middle of its travel, spreading either side of it

<!-- key: rst-scope-intens -->
Reset: Intensity, 0–1

### Trigger

<!-- key: scope-trig-cell -->
Trigger — LEVEL is the value the signal must cross, in the SLOPE direction, for a sweep to start; set it inside the waveform or nothing triggers. SLOPE, the buttons at the right: + triggers on the signal rising through the level, − on it falling. MODE, the buttons at the left: AUTO sweeps anyway when nothing crosses, so silence shows a baseline; NORM sweeps only on a trigger and holds the last trace otherwise.

### Trigger level

<!-- key: scope-trig-led -->
Trigger level — type a value or turn the knob

<!-- key: rst-scope-trig -->
Reset: Trigger level

### Volts/div

<!-- key: scope-volts-read-cell -->
Volts/div — how much signal one division of height is worth, detented 1-2-5. A trace off the top of the screen means this is set too fine. Under it the two INPUT mini knobs: 1 and 2, what channel 1 and channel 2 are fed from — the rack's L or R, Gen 1 to 4 alone, or the model's x, y, z or distance from the center — named on the face, top left. The reset puts all three back.

### Input 1

<!-- key: scope-in1 -->
Input 1 — what channel 1 is fed from

### Input 2

<!-- key: scope-in2 -->
Input 2 — what channel 2 is fed from

### INPUT positions (both knobs, every scope)

- <!-- key: scope-in=0 --> RACK L — the left of the rack's signal, as the Mixer's RACK L row makes it
- <!-- key: scope-in=1 --> RACK R — the right of the rack's signal
- <!-- key: scope-in=2 --> CAP L — the capture's left, wherever the Mixer sends it: a probe on the input
- <!-- key: scope-in=3 --> CAP R — the capture's right
- <!-- key: scope-in=4 --> GEN 1 — Gen 1 alone, as its knobs set it: a probe on its output, pinned anywhere or nowhere
- <!-- key: scope-in=5 --> GEN 2 — Gen 2 alone, as its knobs set it
- <!-- key: scope-in=6 --> GEN 3 — Gen 3 alone, as its knobs set it
- <!-- key: scope-in=7 --> GEN 4 — Gen 4 alone, as its knobs set it
- <!-- key: scope-in=8 --> MODEL X — the model's x: a probe on Model Out, pinned anywhere or nowhere
- <!-- key: scope-in=9 --> MODEL Y — the model's y, from Model Out
- <!-- key: scope-in=10 --> MODEL Z — the model's z, from Model Out
